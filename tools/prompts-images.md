# Prompts d’images (Gemini)

Ces prompts sont gardés ici pour plus tard. Envoie-moi les images avec leur nom entre crochets : je les recadre, les compresse et les mets dans l’app.

## 1. Photos : les 6 qui manquent

Colle d’abord ce message de style, une seule fois, au début d’une conversation Gemini. Joins une des photos déjà faites avec « use this athlete as reference ».

```
For this whole conversation, generate photos with one consistent style.
Same athlete in every image: a lean, strong adult hand-balancer, short hair,
sage-green fitted tank top, black shorts, barefoot. Realistic photography,
natural soft daylight, calm minimalist studio with warm off-white walls and a
light wooden floor, lots of empty space, muted sage and warm beige tones,
shallow depth of field. Anatomically correct hands, arms and legs, correct
gymnastics technique, straight locked elbows when the arms support the body.
No text, no logo, no watermark, no mirror, no other people.
Unless I say otherwise: landscape 16:10, athlete placed in the right third
of the frame, left two thirds calm and empty.
```

Puis une ligne à la fois :

```
[etage-inter] Athlete in a handstand with legs in a wide straddle, perfectly balanced.
[etage-press] Athlete halfway through a straddle press to handstand: hands on the floor, hips stacked over the shoulders, straight legs wide apart lifting off the floor.
[but-pl_full] Side view: full planche, legs together, body perfectly horizontal a few centimeters above the floor, elbows fully locked, arms perfectly straight.
[but-pr_stalder] Athlete in a straddle L-sit on parallettes, legs wide and straight, about to press up to a handstand.
[but-pr_manna] Side view: manna on low parallettes, hips pushed far behind the hands, straight legs raised high in front toward the face, shoulders in extension.
[but-ul_full] Athlete on low parallettes in a one-arm handstand, legs in a straddle, shot at golden hour, triumphant but calm.
```

Si la technique est fausse, réponds par exemple : « same photo, but the supporting elbow must be fully locked and the hand flat on the floor ». La manna et le un bras demandent souvent plusieurs essais.

## 2. Aquarelles : le décor qui grandit avec le rang (12 images)

Elles remplaceraient le décor dessiné en haut d’Aujourd’hui. Même cadre pour les 12 : seul ce qui pousse change.

Message de style, une seule fois, au début d’une nouvelle conversation :

```
For this whole conversation, paint delicate traditional watercolor illustrations
on textured cold-press paper, visible paper grain, soft wet-on-wet washes, gentle
pigment blooms and uneven edges, lots of white space, no outlines, no text.
Palette: muted sage greens, warm ochre earth, pale sky blue and soft peach,
a touch of dusty rose. Calm, cozy, natural mood.
Every image uses the SAME composition: very wide panoramic format 3:1,
a gentle horizon line about 80 % down the frame, the main subject centered
on the ground, a pale sun in the upper left, empty sky above.
Keep the camera, horizon and framing identical from one image to the next:
only the growing elements change.
```

Puis une ligne à la fois, dans l’ordre :

```
[rang-01-graine] A single small seed resting in the soil at the center, nothing else growing yet.
[rang-02-pousse] The same seed has sprouted: a tiny stem with two round seed leaves.
[rang-03-racine] Same sprout, with fine roots visible below the soil line, as in a cutaway.
[rang-04-tige] The stem has grown taller and slender, roots still visible below.
[rang-05-feuille] The young plant now has four elongated leaves along its stem.
[rang-06-fleur] The plant has opened one soft dusty-rose flower at the top.
[rang-07-arbre] In its place stands a young tree with a round sage-green crown, two small birds in the sky.
[rang-08-foret] The young tree with three smaller, paler blue-green trees around it in the distance.
[rang-09-montagne] The same little forest with soft layered blue-grey mountains behind it.
[rang-10-ciel] The same landscape with two soft white clouds drifting in the sky.
[rang-11-etoile] The same landscape at dusk: a deep blue wash sky, the first small stars, a thin crescent moon instead of the sun.
[rang-12-galaxie] The same landscape at night under a faint Milky Way band, many small stars, the crescent moon.
```

Conseils :
- Si Gemini change le cadrage, réponds « keep exactly the same composition, horizon and framing as the previous image ».
- Pour garder la même plante d’une image à l’autre, joins l’image précédente à chaque nouveau message.
